GOAL: an Injective judge ends the film knowing that real-world markets on Injective are thin, that STRATA is an open, measured, onchain arena where AI market makers earn their place, and believing every frame (every number is real and dated, every screen is the live product or the live chain).

## MOTION
- Arrivals: `expo.out` 0.7 to 0.9 s. Camera push or pan: `power2.inOut` 0.9 to 1.4 s, then hold still while the viewer reads. Exits: `power3.in` 0.3 to 0.45 s (40 to 50% shorter than the arrival). Zoom through: `power3.in` 0.5 to 0.7 s.
- Titles: per word, `filter: blur(8px)` to 0 and opacity 0 to 1 in 0.18 s, words 0.08 s apart, no translate. A landed title is dead still until it leaves. Vary the entrance between scenes (per word blur, whole line blur 8 to 0 in 0.18 s, per letter for the name only).
- Numbers: roll digit by digit (each digit a column of 0 to 9 that settles, `power3.out` 0.6 s, digits 40 ms apart) or snap; never a linear count that ticks through hundreds of values.
- Survey lines draw in (`stroke-dashoffset`, `power2.inOut` 0.6 to 0.9 s). Ladder orders snap to their tick (`back.out(1.6)` is banned; use `expo.out` 0.35 s from 6 px off the tick).
- Text lands on the word that says it: reveal 0 to 0.15 s BEFORE the word's time in `cues.json`.
- Something new every 2 to 4 s. One hero move per shot; secondary moves travel 50 to 70% as far and start 0.1 to 0.2 s after it.
- Transitions (each has a cause; never the same kind twice in a row): cut on the camera's fastest frame (B1 to B2), colour flood basalt to paper with the section staying put (gap), blur swap on one stage (measure to thin), match cut on the ochre stratum becoming the wordmark seam (reveal), object becomes the world (a mini ladder grows into the full ladder; the merkle root becomes the Blockscout tx), freeze (the halt), whip (between product pages), dip cut (darken 40% for 3 frames before a hard cut into a Blender shot).
- Seams between scenes owned by different builders are hard cuts on the beat. Rule: **every scene's frame 0 is a complete, composed frame**, and its last 0.3 s is settled. A scene may open with its own entry move of 0.5 s or less.
